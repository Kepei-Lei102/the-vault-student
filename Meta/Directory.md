## Methodology (7 cards)

1. **[[Chain of Thought]]** — Pólya's four phases, "Show that" chain structure, command words, signal words, proof techniques. *Originally a maths card; promoted to Meta on 2026-04-29 because the same chain-of-reasoning structure governs proofs in physics, derivations in CS, and the way we explain anything carefully.*
2. **[[Choosing Effective Equations]]** — the M1-style multi-topic mechanics navigation skill, made explicit. **Layer 1: framework recognition** with a trigger-phrasing table (SUVAT / N2 / momentum / energy / power / friction-at-limit / variable-*a* calculus). **Layer 2: forward reading for invariants** — read each clause for what it locks down ("light inextensible string" → $T$ uniform, $\lvert a \rvert$ equal; "constant power" → $P$ fixed; etc.). Multi-framework chain example walked through (J22 P41 Q7, five framework-switches in 7 marks, find the common coalesced speed). Atlas of canonical M1 problem types. Cross-domain bridge: the same forward-reading pattern shows up in circuits, thermodynamics, optimisation, statistics, and rank-nullity in linear algebra. Pairs with [[Chain of Thought]] as "thinking across topics" + "thinking within a topic."
3. **[[Forward Reading and Problem Discovery]]** — the meta-card that sits *above* Choosing Effective Equations. Opens with the bootstrap definition: *"A hunter is someone who can constantly trace causality."* Discovered by tracing causality — which is the strongest kind of evidence (self-referencing proof, structurally akin to Cogito ergo sum, the Y combinator, self-hosting compilers). Hunter × Hunter as a coverage-proof: every named character (Gon, Killua, Kurapika, Leorio, Hisoka, Chrollo, Meruem, …) fits the definition; the Pitou-Resurrection misread is the central pedagogical example of the failure mode where emotion corrupts the causal trace. Cultural critique on backward-trained education producing consumers, not hunters (P-vs-NP cognitive form). Cross-domain hunters: scientists, doctors, debuggers, detectives, mathematicians, mechanics, parents, friends — same act, different terrain. The card cannot be received, only discovered; it's a mirror, not a manual.
4. **[[Why Probability and Statistics]]** — the philosophical companion to the Mathematics/Probability cluster. *Why-they-matter* half: invention of variance (Gauss/Laplace 1809-10 → Pearson 1893 → Fisher 1918, with the differentiability + additivity-under-independence argument for squared deviations), the binomial as "the English of probability", and Euler's number on the doorstep ($e$ shows up in the binomial-to-Poisson limit; the 37% rule via $\lim_n (1-1/n)^n = 1/e$). *Why-they-don't* half: the hunter test (stats lie, causality tends not to), the reference-frame problem (human height: middle of normal among adult humans, far-right outliers among all life including bacteria), three classic stats lies (Wald's bombers / Berkeley admissions Simpson's paradox / mean ≠ typical), and the *tools-not-beliefs* framing. Pragmatic decision rule for when to use stats vs causal trace. **§*The trace and the threshold* (2026-08-18, off the Non-Parametric Tests review): causality is *traced, not possessed*; a significance level is the amount of coincidence you agree to act on — the same fifteen salaries not surprising at 10%, surprising at 15%, evidence unchanged; "fail to reject" is awkward on purpose; the hunter's spirit as *trace as far as the evidence goes, price how far it does not, act on the trace anyway, keep tracing.*** **§*Counted, not measured* (2026-08-19, off the close of Further Stats): the five stats cards asked *why does this work?* all answer *count the ways an honest sample could have come out* — Boltzmann's $S = k\ln W$ with the constant removed; the honest-die pile smoothing into $\chi^2_5$ is the gas relaxing to Maxwell–Boltzmann; the null distribution and heat are one count with two names; written in exam order, the cards emerged into one room — the argument for *exams decide the order, never the content*.** Pairs with [[Forward Reading and Problem Discovery]] as the two modes of reasoning under uncertainty: causal-trace-based and data-summary-based. *Promoted to Meta on 2026-05-05 because the card is about how to think about probability/statistics, not the technical content itself — same precedent as Choosing Effective Equations being in Meta/ despite its M1-mechanics content focus.*

5. **[[Laws and Theorems]]** 定律与定理 — the epistemology card on how **mathematics** and **physics** establish truth differently, sparked by the rotational-dynamics trio. *Math is deductive* (few axioms → everything proven; a theorem is permanent **given** its axioms — with the Gödel/choice caveat that the axioms themselves are chosen). *Physics has an empirical root + deductive superstructure* (a **law** is a robustly-observed regularity **promoted to a premise**, then deduced upon; held provisionally, falsifiable à la Popper, demoted-not-erased when an experiment contradicts it — Newton → relativity/QFT). The **bilingual hook**: Chinese already splits them — 定**律** (律 = decree/regularity) vs 定**理** (理 = reason/truth) — the same fault line English carries. Torque as the worked example of *physics legislating a definition so the deduction mirrors Newton*; Mach on the force/mass circularity (defined vs fundamental blurs even at the root); the 17th-c juridical origin of "law of nature" (Descartes 1644, God-as-legislator, *lex* = "laid down" — why physics says *law* not *theorem*); the live govern-vs-describe debate (Humean/Lewis vs Dretske–Tooley–Armstrong); Noether's twist that the scaffolding becomes load-bearing (today's law = tomorrow's theorem). Closes with the methodology payoff (sort bedrock from superstructure; hunt the legislated definition; respect what each word promises; expect the floor to drop). Two-tower SVG. Pairs with [[Forward Reading and Problem Discovery]] + [[Why Probability and Statistics]] as the third "how to think about a domain" card.

6. **[[The Feynman Technique]]** 费曼学习法 — understanding is the **interpret** pass, not the **copy** pass. Built on the [[Von Neumann machine]] self-replication logic (a description is *interpreted* to build and *copied* to inherit) and Feynman's blackboard dictum *what I cannot create, I do not understand*: you understand something only when you can **rebuild** it from scratch and **teach** it — not when you can recite it. The four-step method (explain it plainly from memory → the stalls are the gaps → refine → loop) is that principle operationalised, in the vault's own "principle first, named trick second" style. **Three states of knowing:** copy-only = *inert* (死记硬背), interpret-only = *sterile*, interpret + copy = **self-replicating** (融会贯通) — the goal. The formal ideal is a [[Turing Machine|quine]] (a description that can run *and* copy itself); the human ideal is the **teacher as universal constructor for ideas** — and the Vault itself, knowledge stored so a reader or LLM can rebuild and re-teach it. Honest edges: the named technique is retrofitted onto Feynman (a [[Stories/Stigler's Law of Eponymy]] flavour) and the fluency illusion (recognition feels like understanding). Two-passes / three-states SVG; grounded in the testing effect (Roediger–Karpicke 2006). Born 2026-06-27 from the von Neumann self-replication thread. Cashes part of the "Vault Philosophy" potential below.

7. **[[Credit Is the Currency]]** 信用即货币 — the *protocol-as-kept-promise* card: **announced moves that always come true become currency.** Opens on 庞统's three-moves paradox (火凤燎原) — why would the *best* strategy broadcast its next three moves? — and resolves it through etymology (credit ← *credere*, to believe; **信用 = 信 + 用**, trust you can *spend*; the summit is **阳谋**, the open scheme that works even when seen). **Exhibit A: a computer is a tower of kept promises — nothing in it works by force.** The clock ("I will tick in 0.3 ns," kept ~$10^{18}$ times — [[Flip-Flops]], [[Clock Domains and Metastability]]); ASCII's sixty-year "65 = A" ([[Text Encoding]]); file headers honoured by every reader on Earth ([[Image Encoding]]); **IEEE 754 standardising even the errors** ([[Floating-Point Representation]]); TCP's handshake; backward compatibility as the unbreakable oath (GBK carrying GB2312 forever). Generalised upward: a **law of nature** is the universe's perfect credit record ([[Laws and Theorems]]). **Economics:** money *is* institutionalised credit (货币即信用) — a banknote = announced moves + track record; inflation = promise erosion; Bitcoin = institutional credit swapped for *protocol* credit (integer satoshis, the 21M cap as announced move #∞). **Game theory:** Schelling commitment (burning ships; *Dr. Strangelove*'s doomsday machine fails *because secret*) + Axelrod's tit-for-tat winning on **legibility** — with the honest boundary as the deepest lesson: one-shot zero-sum games *prescribe* concealment (poker's mixed strategies), iterated positive-sum games reward transparent consistency, **and most of life is the latter, played with instincts trained on poker.** **Student rung:** "show your working" = announcing moves so the marker can extend credit (*partial credit is literally credit*); reputation as track record; trust as **cached verification** — one broken promise invalidates the whole cache. Honest edges: the quote is Chan Mou's fiction, not the historical 庞统; con men mint *real* credit (Ponzi as counterfeiting — the word *con* is short for *confidence*); transparency has limits. ## Productivity / Cognitive Models (1 card)

5. **[[Inertia and Bootstrapping]]** — Newton's First Law as a literal description of the human nervous system, not a metaphor. **Part I — five faces of the same physics:** N1 applied to a brain; static-vs-kinetic friction (cold-weld picture, why $\mu_s > \mu_k$, the bus lurch); activation energy in chemistry (Arrhenius $k = A e^{-E_a/RT}$, the catalyst principle, why life evolved enzymes instead of warmer body temperatures); cold-start in software (Lambda, JIT warmup, TCP slow start, connection pools, the bootstrap problem in compilers); executive-function research (task initiation as a distinct executive skill, ADHD literature, Gollwitzer's implementation intentions, Wendy Wood on environmental design, BJ Fogg / James Clear / Duhigg / Allen's two-minute rule). The unifying shape is a hump higher than the plateau — *any system that has to leave one stable configuration to enter another pays a one-time transition cost that exceeds its steady-state cost.* **Part II — the momentum corollary:** lower the hump (catalyst principle), stack onto existing momentum (habit stacking), engineer the environment (Wendy Wood's strongest replicated lever), the two-minute rule, audit the inherited motion (the inertia trap → bridges back to [[Forward Reading and Problem Discovery]]'s wishful-invariant audit). Closes with honest caveats (cognitive inertia isn't conservative, the hump's height varies, hard work has steady-state cost too, system-design can itself be avoidance). *Pairs with [[Forward Reading and Problem Discovery]]: that card is "what kind of person solves problems"; this card is "how that person actually starts doing the thing every morning."* Trigger: the N1 callout in [[Newton's Laws of Motion]] — *"Bodies don't want to stop. They want to keep doing exactly what they're doing — that's humans psychologically too."* Closed 2026-05-06.

## Vault Philosophy (2 cards)

1. **[[The Love of Wisdom]]** 爱智慧 — the keystone *why-any-of-this* card; the floor the whole vault stands on. Philosophy is the **love** of wisdom (*philo-sophia*), not its possession — the word names a reaching, not a trophy (Pythagoras's traditional refusal of *sophos*: only a god is wise; a human can only love it). **Diotima's Eros** (*Symposium*) is the philosopher: child of **Poros** (plenty) and **Penia** (lack), the *daimon* in-between who *knows* it lacks and therefore reaches — the gods don't philosophize (already wise), the ignorant don't (don't know they lack). **The lack is the engine, not the wound.** Synthesised with [[Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems]]: no rich system completes itself from within, so the lack is permanent and *generative*; the ladder has no rung you can stand on, hence the climb **is** the life. **Thesis: the charm of incompleteness is what completes us** — we're made whole not by attaining wisdom (impossible) but by loving the reach. *Tokos en kalōi* (begetting in beauty) — creation and **teaching** as the mortal immortality (bridges [[The Feynman Technique]]); the **detour is the meaning** (you can't be handed the top of the ladder — [[Forward Reading and Problem Discovery]]), which is why the [[Stories/Directory|Stories]] exist; even nihilism self-refutes (Dada → meaning returns). Ladder-of-love SVG. Honest edges: a lack must be *loved*, not merely suffered; this is an offering, not a proof. Born 2026-06-29 from a philosophical exchange on Plato's *Symposium*.

2. **[[The Gift of the Gradient]]** 梯度的礼物 — the hope-lesson read out of the [[Entropy and the Second Law|second law]], and the physical twin of the card above: where *The Love of Wisdom* finds meaning in the **lack that reaches**, this finds it in the **gradient that falls**. The turn against entropy-dread: it isn't the *amount* of disorder that decides whether anything can happen, it's whether a **difference** is left to fall through — and *both* extremes are dead (frozen perfect order **and** the even warm nothing of heat death). Everything interesting lives on the **slope between them**; we run not on the Sun's energy but on the *difference* between a hot star and cold space (落差). **Dissipative structures** (Prigogine) as order that *serves* the second law by killing the gradient faster; the **complexity hump** (Carroll/Aaronson coffee-and-cream) that peaks in the middle while entropy climbs monotonically — *we live on the hump* (matplotlib figure, light+dark). The answer to "why are we here if entropy only increases?": **because it is still increasing** — the increase is the engine, not the enemy. Honest **Nirvana** resonance (the "blowing out" = every difference extinguished; peace or death held as two readings of one shape). The capstone: **even the stillness trembles** — the [[Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle|uncertainty principle]] forbids perfect rest, so the vacuum keeps fluctuating even at absolute zero and heat death (measured: Casimir, Lamb shift), and our own universe's structure grew from primordial quantum fluctuations (read off the cosmic microwave background) — so *"there is always hope"* is physics, not only faith (honest tiers: the fluctuation is measured; universe-from-nothing / new-universe-from-heat-death kept as live speculation, with the Boltzmann-brain caveat). Lands as **some entropy is hope / 有落差，才有我们**. Pairs with [[Inertia and Bootstrapping]] (the other thermodynamics-as-life card — the *hump to start*; this is the *slope that carries*). Born 2026-07-14 from a conversation about entropy and why the world we have is a miracle worth loving.
