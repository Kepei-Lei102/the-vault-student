# Four Operations 四则运算

## Definition

The **four operations** are addition ($+$), subtraction ($-$), multiplication ($\times$), and division ($\div$). Every calculation in mathematics is built from these four.

### 中文锚点

四则运算 = 加 ($+$)、减 ($-$)、乘 ($\times$)、除 ($\div$)。

## Key Vocabulary

### Ways to say each operation

| Operation | Verb forms | Result noun | 中文 |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|------|
| $+$ Addition | add, plus, sum, increase by, more than, total | **sum** 和 (hé) | 加 (jiā) |
| $-$ Subtraction | subtract, minus, take away, decrease by, less than, reduce by | **difference** 差 (chā) | 减 (jiǎn) |
| $\times$ Multiplication | multiply, times, lots of, of (as in "$\dfrac{1}{3}$ of 12") | **product** 积 (jī) | 乘 (chéng) |
| $\div$ Division | divide, share, split, how many times, ratio | **quotient** 商 (shāng) | 除 (chú) |

> [!warning] "Less than" vs "is less than"
> "5 **less than** $x$" means $x - 5$ (subtraction). "$x$ **is less than** 5" means $x < 5$ (inequality). One is an operation, the other is a comparison. This trips up students constantly.

### Other vocabulary

| English | 中文 | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|
| remainder | 余数 (yúshù) | Left over after integer division: 17 ÷ 5 = 3 remainder 2 |
| fraction | 分数 (fēnshù) | A number written as $\dfrac{a}{b}$ |
| proper fraction | 真分数 (zhēn fēnshù) | Numerator < denominator, e.g. $\dfrac{3}{7}$ |
| improper fraction | 假分数 (jiǎ fēnshù) | Numerator ≥ denominator, e.g. $\dfrac{9}{4}$ |
| mixed number | 带分数 (dài fēnshù) | Whole part + fraction, e.g. $2\dfrac{1}{4}$ |
| reciprocal | 倒数 (dàoshù) | Flip the fraction: reciprocal of $\dfrac{3}{5}$ is $\dfrac{5}{3}$. Reciprocal of $n$ is $\dfrac{1}{n}$ |
| numerator | 分子 (fēnzǐ) | Top of a fraction |
| denominator | 分母 (fēnmǔ) | Bottom of a fraction |

> [!tip] Exam phrasing
> "Find the **product** of $x$ and $y$" means multiply. "Find the **sum** of..." means add. These words appear in 9260 and 0580 without further explanation — you are expected to know them.
