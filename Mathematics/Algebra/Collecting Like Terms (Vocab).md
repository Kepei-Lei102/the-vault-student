# Collecting Like Terms 合并同类项

## Definition

**Like terms** (同类项) are terms with the **same variable(s) raised to the same power(s)**. To **collect** (合并) like terms is to add or subtract their coefficients.

$$\underbrace{3x^2 + 5x^2}_{\text{like terms}} = 8x^2 \qquad \text{but} \quad 3x^2 + 5x \;\text{ cannot be simplified — different powers.}$$

### 中文锚点

把口袋里的硬币倒在桌上数一数：一元的归一堆，五角的归一堆，没有人会把一枚一元和一枚五角当成同一种东西加在一起。代数里的同类项就是这个意思。$3x^2$ 是三个 $x^2$，$5x^2$ 是五个 $x^2$，数的是同一种东西，所以合起来是 $8x^2$；$3x^2$ 和 $5x$ 是两种不同的东西，因为 $x^2$ 和 $x$ 是不同的量，就像面值不同的硬币，只能各归各的堆。合并同类项，说到底就是把每一种东西各数一遍。

| unlike terms | 非同类项 | Cannot be combined |
| simplify | 化简 (huàjiǎn) | Collect all like terms to shorten the expression |
| collect | 合并 (hébìng) | Group like terms together |

---

## WHY It Works

Like terms represent the same "unit." $3x^2$ means "three lots of $x^2$" and $5x^2$ means "five lots of $x^2$." Adding them is the same as $3$ apples $+ \; 5$ apples $= 8$ apples. You can't add apples and oranges — that's why $3x^2 + 5x$ stays as it is.

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Combining unlike terms:** $3x + 2x^2 \neq 5x^2$. The powers must match.
2. **Forgetting the sign belongs to the term:** In $5x - 3y + 2x$, the $-3y$ stays negative. Result: $7x - 3y$.
3. **Treating $x$ and $xy$ as like terms:** They are not — $xy$ has an extra factor of $y$.

## Exam Notes

### Cambridge 0580 Extended

**Syllabus ref:** E2.2 #1 (Algebraic manipulation): "simplify expressions by collecting like terms", with the syllabus's own gloss — *"Simplify means give the answer in its simplest form"* — and its own calibration of the tiers: the Core example is $2a + 3b + 5a - 9b$, the Extended one is $2a^2 + 3ab - 1 + 5a^2 - 9ab + 4$ (like terms now include $a^2$ and $ab$ as separate species). A sub-step of almost every algebra question rather than a question of its own.

### OxAQA 9260

**Syllabus ref:** A4: "collecting like terms and expanding brackets" (core), products of two or three binomials (extension) — collection and expansion share one row on this board.

### Where it is *not* examined

Named nowhere beyond IGCSE; from 0606 upward "simplify" assumes it without comment.
